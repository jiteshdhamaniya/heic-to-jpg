# Third-party software

The MIT license in this repository covers the application code and its synthetic test fixture. Dependencies retain their own licenses; see their license files in `node_modules` after `npm ci`.

- Electron — MIT; its bundled Chromium and other components include additional notices distributed with Electron.
- electron-builder — MIT (development and packaging).
- heic-convert — ISC.
- heic-decode — ISC.
- libheif-js — LGPL-3.0, wrapping libheif and its codec dependencies. See the dependency's bundled notices and upstream source at https://github.com/catdad-experiments/libheif-js and https://github.com/strukturag/libheif.
- jpeg-js — BSD-3-Clause.
- pngjs — MIT.

Review the dependency licenses when redistributing a compiled application. The project's MIT license does not replace their terms.
